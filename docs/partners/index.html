潘大謙    藝術總監╱舞作編導╱舞台設計

出生於香港，在台灣及法國接受藝術教育，獲法國國立畢卡迪大學l`Université de Picardie造型藝術碩士。在台灣、香港及歐洲個展十餘次，重要聯展數十次，並積極從事藝術策展活動。2009年作品《閃然》入圍『第7屆台新藝術獎』並角逐年度大獎。2005-2013年擔任團長，2014年起轉任藝術總監並獨立創作《流刑地》、《歐西打街》、《低吹沙》、《水圍花城》、《打南無_漫遊者》、《直線迷宮》、《太倉一粟》、《平流層》、《淨•肉》、《Pure Meat》等劇場作品。

​
Artistic Director    PAN, Ta-Chien    

Born in Hong Kong. Received art education in Taiwan and France.  Master of Art in l`Université de Picardie. Has held several exhibitions around Taiwan,Hong Kong and Europe, and engaging in art-curating activities.  In 2009, Pan’s work “Flashover”  was nominated for “The 7th Taishin Arts Award.”  From 2005 to 2013, Pan worked as the head of Solar Site Dance Theatre.  In 2014, Pan became artistic director of Solar Site, continually creating Solar Site's annual works, such as “In der Strafkolonie”, “Outsider Ally”, “Drifting Sand”, " Submerged Houseleek", “Dynamo_Roaming Away" , “Straight Line Maze" , “A drop in the ocean" , “Stratosphere"  ,“Meat"  ,“Pure Meat" and so on.

程曉嵐    行政總監╱製作人╱共同創作

文化大學舞蹈研究所碩士，1992年起定居南台灣從事舞蹈教育工作。2005年以｢凝聚南台灣優秀創作者，留住南台灣在地舞者」為目標，與藝術家潘大謙共同創立索拉舞蹈空間舞團。2005-2013年擔任索拉舞蹈空間藝術總監，致力於推動團務發展並策劃執行各年度創作發表。2014年起轉任本團行政總監及製作人，連續策畫與執行2010-2024之年度製作。

Producer    CHENG, Hsiao-Lan    
​
Master of Dance in Chinese Culture University. Been Engaging in dance education in southern Taiwan since 1992, with a goal to  “forge and keep artists  and dancers in southern Taiwan.”  In 2005, Cheng found Solar Site Dance Theatre with Pan, Ta-Chien.  From 2005 to 2013, Cheng was the artistic director in Solar Site, dedicated in promotion of the dance company and execution of annual works. Since 2014, Cheng has been working as  executive director and producer of Solar Site and keeping participating in annual works.
