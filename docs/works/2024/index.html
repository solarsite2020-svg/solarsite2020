「人們生存在講究系統機制的物化世界中，肉身被馴化，得以在體制中汲汲營營的符合其規範，而轉頭卻必須在精神中求完滿，補漏生命中的缺失。然而，在這弱肉強食的渾世漩渦裡，有一天，人何嘗不是轉身即成為被反噬的"物"？」＿潘大謙

當代技術化社會一方面解放了個人自由，另一方面鞏固了社會管理的技術，這樣的世界藉由工具理性滿足了個人對自由的本能想望，但去主體性的背後卻依然被巨大的機器絞拌著。
現代文明用「物件」把世界“再現”，人們通過新聞播報的速度，片面得知世界各角落發生的事，用一種清點生活形式及細節的方式，做為認知世界的方法。“人”生存在這個不斷運轉的巨輪中，儼然也成為片段資訊裡被認識的“物”，當失去神秘且繁雜巨大的“物”迎面襲向我們，人們曰之“文明與幸福”。
面對多采多姿的異化荒原，汲汲營營地生活著，無數現代游牧群將精神化為肉身遊蕩著，他們是「歐西打街」中的庶民？福樓拜筆下的「艾瑪」？沙漠邊陲「低吹沙」中的「太倉一粟」？抑或是困在「水圍花城」中的花朵？究竟人們在「平流層」的上空看到了「直線迷宮」？還是來到如熱燃發電機「打南無」般反覆呻吟的「流刑地」？(註)
索拉舞蹈空間近十年的創作，皆在關注現代社會中“人”如何透過“物”審視自己的存在價值，在2023年的《淨․肉》中勾勒了當代人的慾望與匱乏，2024《Pure Meat》則是此肉身在慾望來回的交界中持續反思與救贖。

(註)此段文字整理自潘大謙2013-2023劇場創作的內涵，創作年表如下：
2013《流刑地In der Strafkolonie》、2014《歐西打街Outsider Alley》、2015《低吹沙Drifting Sand》、2016《水圍花城Submerged Houseleek》、2017《打南無_漫遊者Dynamo-Roaming Away》、2018《直線迷宮Straight line maze》、2019《艾瑪Emma》、2021《太倉一粟A drop in the ocean》、2022《平流層Stratosphere》、2023《淨․肉Meat》

​About  “ Pure Meat”
 
The technological society, on one hand, liberates individual freedom, while on the other, it reinforces management techniques. This world satisfies personal desires for freedom, yet behind the scenes, individuals are still entangled in a vast machinery.

People use "objects" to represent the world, and through the rapid dissemination of news, they gain a fragmented understanding of events happening in various corners of the globe. This method of cataloging life and its details becomes a way of perceiving the world. In this constantly turning wheel, humans appear to become "objects" recognized through fragmented information. When the complex and mysterious "object" rushes toward us, it is referred to as "civilization and happiness.

" Confronted with a colorful yet alienating wasteland, people live in relentless pursuit. Count- less modern nomads wander, embodying their spirits. Are the commoners of "Outsider Alley"? Flaubert's "Emma"? The "A drop in the ocean" or a grain of sand in the "Drifting Sand"? Are they the "Submerged Houseleeks"? What do people see in the "Stratosphere" above--a "Straight line maze," or do they arrive "In der Strafkolonie" where the "Dynamo" generator groans like a burning flame?

For nearly a decade, the creative work of Solar Site Dance Company has focused on how individuals reflect on their existential value through "objects" in modern society. In the 2023 piece "Meat," they outlined contemporary desires and deficiencies, while in the 2024 "Pure Meat," the body continues to engage in reflection and seek redemption in the interplay of desires.
