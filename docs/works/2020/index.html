當代資訊化社會使生活貌似多元，然而技術社會一方面解放了個人自由，但另一方面卻鞏固了社會的管治技術，人們在這世界裡利用工具滿足了個人對自由的本能想望，但世界的背後其實依然被巨大的機器絞拌著。
 
「太倉一粟」是比喻個體在無窮量大之前的微不足道，微小的個體身處無窮與蒼涼之境。這令人想到艾未未的觀念藝術作品《葵花籽Sunflower Seeds》，藝術家雇用了1600名工匠做出上億顆陶瓷的葵花籽，每一顆本具有其獨特的形狀外貌，但當被丟置在集體之中，就如同煙塵，渺無聲色；如同那龐大的陶瓷工班一般，沒有自己的姓名與臉孔，但卻成就了一件藝術品。
 
在「太倉」裡，「一粟」被規畫著，被編號著，被歸納著，正如生存在這個充滿數字的時代裡那每一個被碎片化的個體，一粟仍在沉思著嗎……
 
索拉舞蹈空間2020年度創作《太倉一粟A drop in the ocean》，嘗試在全球化思維的經緯框架下，以身體與物對話，探討個體與集體間的疏離卻又無法逃逸的意象。「一粟」存在於無以計數的集體之中如何自處，如何以特殊的意義現身，而「一粟」的特性是否可以被區隔出來並賦予獨自的身分與命運。

About the work <A Drop in the Orean>

​Informatization opens up possibilities in life.  Technology society in one hand frees individuals, and in the other hand solidates management of the society.  People in the world depend on technology to satisfy their desire of freedom.  However, behind the world hides a gigantic machine manipulating.
 
A Drop in The Ocean implies the incessant and desolate situation that negligible individuals live in beneath enormous collective.  Very much like Sunflower Seeds, the conceptual artwork by Ai Weiwei, which displays hundreds of millions ceramic sunflower seeds handmade by 1600 craftsmen.  Each one is unique and different while plain and insignificant in the collective, just as the 1600 craftsmen, without titles and faces, altogether accomplished one art work.
 
In the ocean, “a drop”, labeled and systemized, represents each fragmented individual during the digitalized age.  Could the drop still be thinking?
 
Solar Site Dance Theatre’s new work in 2020, A Drop in The Ocean, through the conversation of bodies and objects, explains the image of doom and estrangement between individuals and collectives under the structure of globalization society.  Could an individual be able to pursue its own character, find its own identity and chooses its own destiny?
