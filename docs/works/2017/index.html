『打南無』一詞來自英文Dynamo，意指發電機。這台其貌不揚的機器，滿身油污，只靠引擎不斷反覆運轉，發出喃喃自語的噪音，卻能靠這種反覆的活動機制，製造巨大能量。

在Dynamo猶如梵音運轉不息的國度裡，棲居著一群又一群的現代遊牧民，如蜉蝣寄生於幾何的荒蕪世界裡，無限拓展彷彿是牧民一切努力的目的。在這巨大的水泥叢林中，大地永遠被塵封於喧囂之外，牧民與土壤所孕育出來的萬物溫存已經成為遙遠的童話。
現代遊牧民他們彼此陌生卻互相聯繫，沒有熱情卻有能量，沒有對生命的敬畏卻膜拜偶像，他們失去遊戲的能力卻有娛樂，沒有抽象因為全然實際，他們以近乎野蠻的精力在奮鬥，活在一個虛構的幸福未來且喃喃自語 ; 他們是現代人，是你也是我，想離開，卻出不去 ; 他們是現代人、是漫遊者…..
​
索拉舞蹈空間2017年度製作<<打南無＿漫遊者Dynamo_ Roaming Away>> 以舞蹈劇場為形式，建構一個當代生存的想像空間，為這暈眩而無能給予解放之力的世界，迎接喃喃自語的能量，為dynamo散熱……。

About the work <Dynamo_Roaming Away>

Dynamo is an electrical generator. This ugly-looking machine covered with oil stains producing enormous electrical power by non-stopping rotary engine. The powering process makes noises like murmuring sounds.
 
In the would that sounds like Buddhist chanting of Dynamo never resets dwelling herds of modern nomad, like tiny parasites inhabiting in the geometric abandoned world. Endlessly expanding borders is their ultimate goal. Among the vast concrete jungle, the earth is never seen; abundant varieties of living creatures have all become a faraway fairytale.
 
The modern nomad are strangers to each other but somehow connected. They produce power but have no passion, worship idols but show no revere to life, have entertainment but lose the capability to enjoy. It is an absolute reality with no room for imagination. They are modern people, just like you and me, trying to break out but fail to. They are modern people, they are nomad.
 
Solar Site Dance theatre’s dance work ‘Dynamo Roaming Away’ in 2017 constructs an imaginary space of modern life though dance theater. In this helpless world, we embrace the power of Dynamo and calm it down.
