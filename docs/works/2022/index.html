《平流層》創作概念來自氣象學名詞Stratosphere，即舊稱的同溫層。它位於大氣「對流層」的上方和「中間層」的下方。不同於大氣底層「對流層」的上下激烈運動，「平流層」中的氣流主要是以水平方向流動，幾乎沒有上下對流，是相對穩定而平靜的空漠。
 
在大氣中的「平流層」猶如一層流動的膜，卻能承托著飛行的載具，它隱喻著一種平和而強大的力量，能延綿流動，雄厚但稀薄。正如人們經歷世間萬事萬物，由外而內、由下而上，從狂暴紋亂的力量渾沌，到水平如鏡般的連綿通達，航向生命的歷程。
 
「平流層」的意象蘊含著一種能量與物質的狀態，在生活中人們既看不到也摸不著，然而潛伏在我們意識的底層，它更清澈地呈現了我們與世界相處的狀態。平流層象徵一種盈滿狀態下的匱乏，空無但充實，它是一個塵世與天空的中介空間。身體是生命在世的載具，亦是唯一的表達工具，如何透過肢體能量展現生命存在的不亢不卑，成為這次《平流層》創作主要關心的議題。
 
在作品中潘大謙和舞者及音樂家共同嘗試透過舞台裝置、空間結構、物件配置，以穿梭、推疊、傾覆等流動狀態，創造一種由上下對抗的張力轉為橫向延伸的穩定挪置，由此身體的流動與反應，去體現從激烈到平順的著力痕跡及人們感知的複合狀態，藉此映照出生命中的恐懼、脆弱，但卻無比堅強。

About the work <Stratosphere>
​
The concept of the work “Stratosphere” comes from meteorology.  The stratosphere is the second layer of atmosphere of Earth, located above the troposphere and below the mesosphere.  Different from the troposphere, the stratosphere, relatively stable and static like vast air desert, consists mostly of horizontal airflow instead of vertical one.

The atmospheric stratosphere is like a thin flowing layer of film capable of carrying flying objects—a metaphor of peaceful yet strong power that extends boundlessly.  Like all things humans have been through in the mortal world.  The power extending from outwards and below, from chaos to tranquility, continues taking us through our life course.

The imagery of the stratosphere is a state containing energy and substance,  which cannot be seen nor touched in real life.  While it lurks at the bottom of our consciousness, clarifying the state between humans and the world.  The stratosphere is a symbol of deficiency in fulfillment, and also an intermediary space between the mortal world and the enormous sky.    Human bodies are carriers of the mortal life and the only expressive tools humans  posses.  To exhibit the humble existence of life through body is the main subject of the work “stratosphere”.
 
Together with dancers and musicians, we try to simulate liquid movements such as streaming, piling and overturning, creating stable transition from vertical tensions to horizontal extensions by using stage equipment, space structure and object configuration.  Reflecting fear, fragility and fortitude in life through body movements and reactions that embody fierce and smooth forces as well as the complexity of human perception.
