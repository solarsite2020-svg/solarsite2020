風是載體沙是客，沙隨風舞人隨影。
​彼此是一個共生的世界，造就大地宿命的擴張。
「低吹沙」是一個氣象學名詞；是風將沙粒吹揚到水平視線以下的高度，但對水平能見度無明顯影響的天氣現象。
一望無際的沙原，遠方景像清澈，眼底下徐風緩緩，牽動沙粒一波接一波。風是載體，沙是乘客，佔據了大地，造就了荒漠，低吹沙是埋伏在自然界裡的欲望暗流，無時無刻緩慢地匍匐前進。不同於沙塵暴的強勢，低吹沙不具備革命的姿態，當微風遇到障礙物，形成風切，沙子或繞道或盤旋，默默地留下了痕跡，等待下一波前進的面容。
人類生存的本能將熱情轉化成創造，用緩慢如「低吹沙」的步伐，靈活而多變的身影，述說著自身的欲望，改變了生存的環境，而世界也總是在遠景清澈的情況下，被漂沙的暗流緩緩地畫向大地，飄向無際的四方。正如「低吹沙」用最柔軟的身段改變了大自然的地貌。
在現代的生活中，「人」看似孤獨，但其實未然。人們憑藉著對生命的熱情與執著，創造出一個共生體的對話世界，以「生之欲」的化身，迫使人們思考與對應；或叛逆或冷漠，人類就站在這熱情、叛逆、冷漠的尖口上，如「低吹沙」般展現其欲望。
「沙隨風舞」。存在者的意志，溫柔卻又強烈的征服大地。本創作是從觀察「低吹沙」如何以輕盈的身段，對自然地貌的刻劃狀態，造就其宿命的擴張，進一步思考現代人面對世界的態度以及生存的樣態。

About the work <Drifting Sand>
​
Drifting sand is a meteorological phenomena; it is when the wind blow the sand blew horizon and has no significan effect on a person’s visibility.
In a vast seemingly endless sand field, where the gentle wind is warm and the distant sight is clear, the sand grains carried by the wind, move ever so slowly wave after wave under the horizon; wind and sand, passenger and carrier, together occupy the land and create a desert. Drifting sand is the desirous undercurrent of the nature lurking and creeping slowly forward. Unlike a violent and destructive sand storm, the drifiting sand does not possess aggressive attitude. When facing an obstacle, the drifiting sand would cut around it and quietly leave a trail of sand behind waiting for the next wave of force to push it forward. 
Humanities' instinct to survive is to transfer passion to creation. By using pace like a drifitng sand and versatile forms that depict self-desire, the humanity slowly change its natural habitat. The world supposingly with a clear future would be curved gradually into pieces by the ever changing under current of the drifting sand, and its shell would be forever changed and lost in oblivion.
Living in the moment, the lonely human compose a realem of conversations with the world in order to exhibit the passion-turned determination to conceive new born. In consequence, this act force human to think and to respond the chanllenge with either rebellious or detached attitude. Standing on the edge of passion, rebellion, and detachment, like the drifting sand express their desire.
Coexisting together, survivers' wells like drifiting sand gentally conquer the world. The piece presented is inspired observing how drifiting sand gracefully shape the land and create its own destiny. The piece examine on how modern human face the world and how they survive it.
