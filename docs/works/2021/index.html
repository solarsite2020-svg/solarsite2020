“記憶遷徙”的命名是由Memories flow轉化而來，flow流動之意，而“Memories flow”則是對流動的生活樣態和生命經驗加以捕捉描述。

索拉舞蹈空間2021以「記憶遷徙」為主題的創作，是以台灣社會變遷與世代交替的概念為背景，關心台灣近兩三個世代中，如何從以土地為本的生存價值和人際關係，因經濟發展和科技介入而急速轉變的生活型態，一種從原鄉情懷的文化記憶轉變為物化疏離的生存狀態。

本次演出將由兩個部分所構成，前半場由王國權運用懷舊的物件，使舞者搭配流暢且深具溫度的肢體語彙，來表達人際交融、和衷共濟的前期生活；而後半場則由程曉嵐帶領年輕世代的舞者們，以懷著未消散的文化記憶為基調，共同表達在科技媒體介入生活，甚至主宰價值的世代裡，無法逃脫且在一切雖近卻遠的異化環境中，人們面對生存的各種荒謬及無奈。

About the work <Memories flow>

Culture is nurtured on the land.  Culture does not migrate; it is humans who migrate.  Cultural memory, which built on the operation of social mechanism, plays a crucial part to collective self identification.  Therefore it maintains consistency through centuries.  Based on the concept of social change in Taiwan from generation to generation, Solar Site Dance Theatre’s work “Memories Flow” observes how the society, where people used to establish strong bonds with hometown and people, rapidly transforms into one of objectification and alienation after economy developed and technology got involved.

The work “Memories Flow” is consisted of two parts, demonstrating diverse aspects and experiences of life.  The first part is a musical-like choreographed performance by professional dancer Wang Kuo-Chuan.  By using nostalgic objects, taste memory and singing, Wang’s fluent and expressive movement vocabulary draws the audience back into the time when people were more united and bonding together and into his own life phases from naive young to sophisticated mature age.The second part, led by Cheng Hsiao-Lan with a younger generation of dancers, expresses the absurd and helplessness humans need to face in the alienated environment impossible to escape from in the era of technology.

Social structure and cultural transformation develop fiercely in Taiwan.  When people enjoy the convenience in life, we somehow feel empty inside standing on this solid ground.  Solar Site Dance Theatre’s 2021 work “Memories Flow” attempts to travel through the river of time, listen among the mist of sorrow and joy, write in the flow of memories, bearing the anticipation of modern civilization in the past and responding to the comprehension of existence after technology has been developed.
