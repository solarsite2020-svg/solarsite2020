「我揮起左手跟你道別，正由於我右手拿著行囊，或者我喜歡用拿扇子的手作為揮別的工具，世界沒有定理，但我感覺到自由。」
 
《直線迷宮》映照一個旅人的故事，一個漂泊在無垠宇宙裡找尋自己卻遺失真理的狀態…...他進入沒有入口也沒有出口的時空，旅途環環相扣沒有轉角卻因果相違，眼前一望無際卻步履生花……。
 
這個創作用一條直線比喻一座迷宮，在迷宮裡的人將面對一個單向度無止境的軸線前進或後退，在那裡，人們找尋出路的一切思維將會翻轉、飛散、消失。
 
人只能活一次，意味著不管如何經營，那只有一次的經驗也只能是唯一的草稿，對生命而言，這是徒勞的努力，最終，人靠自己的思想去追求也歸於徒勞。《直線迷宮》這個創作要表達的不是迂迴的冒險，而是述說當代人在面對一條全然陌生的軸線旅途中關於信念與價值的故事。

About the work <Straight line maze>

“Waving farewell with my left hand because the other one taking the luggage, or simply gesture goodbye with this hand used to hold a fan. There is no theorem in the world, and I sense freedom.”
Straight Line Maze reflects a story of a traveler in a state of self-searching and losing truth in the vast expanse of space. Without neither entrance nor exist during the journey, everything is somehow closely interconnected and inseparable but somehow leading against karma.
 
Using straight line as a metaphor for a maze, people move forward or backward on the endless linear path. Searching for a way out, minds and thoughts go through conflicts and fall apart to nothingness.
 
Human only lives once. No matter how we manage our life, it only presented once and none can rewrite it. Efforts go in vain. Eventually, our mind pursuing turns out empty.  Straight Line Maze don’t expresses the twists and turns of an adventure but a story of belief and value while people in modern society wandering in the total strange linear journey.
