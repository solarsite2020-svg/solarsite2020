人們站在這裡，告訴自己存在著，然後從這裡望向遠方……

傳說中的「花城」是個被四面河水圍困的村落，在那裡資源匱乏且雨季漫長，潮濕多雨的氛圍，孕育出當地居民保有一種陰鬱卻又莊嚴的生命態度。河水圍了個圈，困住了「花城」，面對艱困貧乏的生活條件，「花城」裡的人們彼此相互依賴與扶持，堅毅而無懼地迎接自然的生存法則，他們視死亡為生命的永恆回歸，靜靜的講述著自己的故事。

一段歷史，往往透露着特定的生存環境與生命姿態。被水圍困的「花城」，阻隔了他人，也阻隔了自己，人們眼界所及，是方寸之地，見不著更遠更寬廣的世界，她一方面倔強而自在的展現其自我生命完成的技術，而煙水連天的另一面，卻道出了背後生存的宿命……

《水圍花城》是索拉舞蹈空間2016的年度新作，創作編導潘大謙嘗試從水的流動與生之慾念，作為創作的聯想，並將你我生命中所遭遇過之困頓、衝擊、尊嚴、愛與逃亡……等生存條件，從美學的觀點介入，藉此探討現代人如何面對存在的價值，到底我們擁有過甚麼？又丟失了甚麼？

About the work <Submerged Houseleek>

People stand here, speak to themselves of their existence, and look beyond...

The legendary houseleek city is a village surrounded by a river, where the resource is scarce and the rain season is long. In this watery and rainy environment, local residents have learned to adapt and live in an attitude of gloom but solemnity. In this enclaved territory, despite the difficult and poor conditions, the residents inside depend on each other and support one another, persistently and fearlessly taking on the challenges found in the nature. To them, death is the end of eternal life, and their story will continue to circulate, slowly and quietly. 

History often reveals a unique living environment and a life style. This waterlogged city isolates herself from the outside world. What people enclosed can see is nothing but an inch of land. The vast world is completely obstructed from their sight. She, in one perspective, tenaciously and freely expresses the self-fulfilling skills to the world, while in the other perspective, where the water and sky becomes one, speaks of her fate of existence.

2016《Submerged Houseleek》created by Solar Site Dance Theatre is an attempt to use the representation of water flow and desires in life to aesthetically introduce the experiences of hardship, shock, dignity, love and escape in our life, in order to explore how modern people experience their existence? What exactly have we owned? And what have we lost?
