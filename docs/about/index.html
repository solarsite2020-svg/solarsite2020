關於索拉舞蹈空間

​​​​「索拉舞蹈空間」成立於2005年。「索拉」一詞，意念源自Solar， Solar Site意指充滿太陽能量的空間，同時也意味索拉舞蹈空間的目標，是以正面向上的力量持續關注當代社會及藝術創作的議題。
索拉舞蹈空間近年來的創作方向，皆在探討生命存在於全球化的步伐和資本邏輯秩序運作下的無奈，以及現代人在此既親暱又疏離的荒謬社會中，所展現的存在狀態。成立以來，已發表2007《光之舞》、2008《Relight-光之舞2》、2009《Bingo！Be In GO》、2010《無關係的關係》、2011《感覺良好》、2012《〝離〞生活近一點》、2013《流刑地》、2014《歐西打街》、2015《低吹沙》、2016《水圍花城》、2017《打南無_漫遊者》、 2018《直線迷宮》、2019《艾瑪》、2020《太倉一粟》、2021《記憶遷徙》、2022《平流層》、2023《淨•肉》、2024《Pure Meat》，2012~2017入選文化部分級獎助團隊、2018~2021獲選「高雄市傑出演藝團隊(舞蹈類)」、2022入選「國藝會Taiwan Top年度獎助團隊(舞蹈類)」、2023-2024獲選「高雄市傑出演藝團隊(跨領域類)」。未來，我們仍將秉持當代藝術創作的精神，持續發展跨界合作的創作能量，並且積極辦理表演藝術相關推廣活動及藝文講座，為南台灣現有藝文環境投注心力。

About Solar Site Dance Theatre

About Solar Site Dance Theatre
< Solar Site Dance Theatre > was established in 2005. “Solar Site” means a space full of energy from the sun, which is also the spirit of Solar Site Dance Theatre. Our choreographies positively focus on collaboration between modern society issues and artistic creations.
In recent years, Solar dance company focuses choreographies on rediscovering the helpless feeling of living under the fast pace of globalization and capitalization. Since established, the company also tries to show an existential state of modern human in this intimate and alienated society. We have performed several pieces: Relight(2007), Relight II(2008), Bingo!Be In Go(2009), Relation of Relationless(2010), No matter Feels Good(2011), Taking a step closer to LIVE anyhow…(2012), In der Strafkolonie(2013), Outsider Alley(2014), Drifting Sand(2015), Submerged Houseleek(2016) ,Dynamo_Roaming Away(2017) ,Straight Line Maze(2018) ,Emma(2019),A drop in the ocean(2020) ,Memories flow(2021) ,Stratosphere(2022)  ,Meat(2023) and Pure Meat(2024) .
We have been selected as one of the representing groups in “Performance Troupe Classifying Award Plan”by Ministry of Culture(2012-2017)  ,  “Excellent performance Arts Team” by Kaohsiung (2018-2021 ,2023-2024) and "TAIWAN TOP Performing Arts Team" (2022).
Solar Site Dance Theatre has become the most active contemporary dance company in Kaohsiung, Taiwan. In the future, we’ll uphold the spirit of contemporary arts and keep developing creative energy of cross-boundary cooperation. We’ll actively manage extensional performing arts programs and lectures so that we can further contribute to current art environment in southern Taiwan.
